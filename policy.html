Store Policies & Customer Terms

Last updated: August 2026. Please read our order, delivery and refund guidelines.

1. Ordering & Delivery Policy

* Direct WhatsApp Ordering: Jab aap website par order place karte hain, order details seedha humare kitchen WhatsApp par receive hoti hain aur admin dashboard me record hoti hain.

* Estimated Delivery Time: Standard delivery time 30 se 45 minutes hota hai. Live status track page par available rehta hai.

2. Payments & UPI Security

* Accepted Methods: Cash on Delivery (COD), Cash on Counter, and Instant Online UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).

* No Extra Charges: Zero platform commission, zero surge fees. Jo price menu me hai, wahi final bill hota hai.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

* Order Cancellation: Food freshly bake kiya jata hai, isliye order WhatsApp par confirm hone ke 5 minute ke andar hi cancel kiya ja sakta hai.

* Wrong or Damaged Item: 100% instant replacement ya refund initiated within 15 minutes of intimation.

4. Privacy & Data Protection

* Customer data kisi third party ke sath share nahi kiya jata hai.
